## 1. The Core Story (What We're Talking About)

Meta Andromeda is a **personalized ads retrieval engine** that fundamentally changes how ads get matched to people. Retrieval is the *first* step in Meta's ad recommendation pipeline — the moment where the system narrows tens of millions of ad candidates down to a few thousand relevant ones.

**The three things everyone needs to understand:**

1. **A 10,000x jump in model complexity.** Andromeda runs on a hardware-software stack combining Meta's own MTIA silicon and NVIDIA Grace Hopper (GH200) Superchips — enabling machine learning at the retrieval stage that simply wasn't possible before.
2. **Audience-first → Creative-first.** The old game was targeting the right *people*. The new game is having the right *creative*, because Andromeda matches the most relevant ad creative to each individual based on thousands of behavioral signals in near real-time.
3. **Creative volume becomes the growth lever.** Because the engine evaluates creative at the "Entity ID" level (a visual pattern, not just a file), advertisers who feed it more diverse, high-quality creative give it more to work with.

**One-sentence hook:** *Meta just made your creative your targeting.*
