# War Room — 30-Day Content Sprint

> **What this is:** A ready-to-run 30-day publishing calendar built from the
> [POV Library](war-room-pov-library.md) (Rounds 1–3). Every slot maps to a POV, a proof point,
> and a format. Byline: **War Room** (roles internal, no personal names published).
>
> **Parameters:** 30 days · **2×/week** (Tue + Thu) · **LinkedIn-led** (company page + role bylines)
> · 1 monthly newsletter. Set your start date and the weekly dates fill in from there.

---

## Channels & weekly rhythm
- **Primary:** LinkedIn — company page, amplified by the relevant role's profile.
- **Cadence:** 2 posts/week. **Tuesday = the "big take"** (article/post that can carry a carousel); **Thursday = lighter format** (carousel or short post + quote graphic).
- **Every post ships with a paired quote graphic** from the [Quote Graphics Kit](war-room-quote-graphics-kit.md).
- **Monthly:** one newsletter (Week 4) that repackages the month's flagship take.

## Sequencing logic
- **Open on the strongest hook** (#1 the AI flagship, "variation not differentiation") — provocative, timely, and needs no clearance → ships day one.
- **Flagship mid-month** (#3 "ROAS is the floor") once numbers are cleared — the month's centerpiece, echoed by the Week-4 newsletter.
- **Alternate heavy/light** — Tuesday big take, Thursday lighter format — so production stays sustainable at 2×/week.
- **Spread the pillars** — no two consecutive posts from the same pillar.
