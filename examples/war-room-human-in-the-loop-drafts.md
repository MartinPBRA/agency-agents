## 1 · Series launch post (pin this)
**LinkedIn post — introduces the concept**
> Every agency is telling you they use AI. So are we. Here's what we're actually going to show you.
>
> We use AI every day. It speeds up the reps — analysis, variations, execution. What it can't do is understand your business, sit in the room with your customer, or be accountable for your growth.
>
> That part is still a person's job. Ours.
>
> So we're starting a series: **Human in the Loop.** No polished "AI transformed everything" theatre — just our team showing the real work. Where AI helps. Where we overrule it. And why the judgment call is the part you're actually paying for.
>
> AI does the reps. Our people make the calls.
>
> First one drops this week. 👇
>
> — War Room

## 3 · "AI drafted. I decided." (finished LinkedIn post)
> AI wrote 40 versions of this campaign. We shipped none of them.
>
> To be clear — the 40 were good. Generated in minutes, clean, on-format. A year ago that's a full afternoon of work, done before lunch.
>
> But every one optimized for the wrong thing: the metric the platform likes, not the outcome the client actually needs.
>
> So [Team member] did the part the machine can't — looked at the client's real goal, and made a different call. The winning concept wasn't in the 40. It came from understanding the business behind the brief.
>
> That's the job now. AI does the reps. People make the calls.
>
> AI helps us. It doesn't do the job.
>
> — War Room

## Production notes
- **Keep it real, not glossy** — phone-shot screen + talking head beats an overproduced ad. The authenticity *is* the trust (the Thrive lesson).
- **Every episode needs a real override** — a genuine "AI suggested X, we did Y, here's why." No staged decisions.
- **Consistent end card** — "Human in the loop. Always." + logo, every time, so the series is recognizable.

## Open decision (blocks the video, not the posts)
- **Real names + faces vs. role-based?** Recommended: real people for this series (the whole point is human connection). The written posts above work either way — swap `[Team member]` for a name or a role.
- **Confirm who opts in** to being on camera, and pick the Episode 1 task/topic.
