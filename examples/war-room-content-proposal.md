# War Room — Content System Proposal

> **One-page overview** of the content system built from the Social Calendar Plan survey. This is the
> client-facing summary; each line links to the working document. Byline standard: **War Room**.

---

## The idea in one line
Turn the team's own expertise — captured through the Social Calendar Plan survey — into a defensible, ownable content engine, where every post carries a sharp point of view backed by proof, in a voice that's recognizably War Room.

## What we're solving
- Survey answers were *topics* ("Evolving with AI," "Programmatic and DOOH"), not publishable *takes*.
- "We use BigQuery / we do DOOH" is what every agency says — no differentiation.
- No documented voice, so content risked sounding like generic agency filler.

## The engine behind it
- **Deep Response Engine** — ladders thin survey answers into defensible POVs.
- **Voice System** — the signature *"not X — Y"* antithesis, concession-then-pivot, named receipts, business-outcome language.
- **Voice Rubric** — every draft scores 4/5+ before it ships.
- **Answer → Voice Pipeline** — each new survey batch converts straight into finished, on-voice content.

## Content pillars
1. **DOOH, Demystified** · 2. **Programmatic in Practice** · 3. **AI, For Real** · 4. **Proof & Partnership** · 5. **Paid Media Truths (No-BS)**

## Signature takes (the voice, proven)
- "Great marketing scales success. It doesn't create it."
- "BigQuery didn't make us faster. Killing manual reporting did."
- "Every platform recommendation should come with a warning label: based on algorithms, not your business context."

## Guardrails
- **Markets:** Canada + San Francisco / Bay Area.
- **Excluded:** vaping, gambling, regulated industries; no recycling the same themes.
- **Cleared:** Meta and TikTok can be named publicly.
- **Attribution:** War Room byline (roles internal; no personal names published).

## What we need from you to go live
1. **Proof numbers** — 2–3 cleared figures (SkiBig3 reporting hours saved, any DOOH lift %, a $50K-audit reallocation result). Lifts several clusters to a perfect 5/5.
2. **Client-story clearance** — confirm which named client cases we can reference.
3. **Launch order** — recommended: open with *"Paid media can't fix a bad product,"* then alternate Paid Media Truths and AI clusters weekly.

## Cadence (once live)
1 quote graphic + 1 carousel or post per week; 1 case study + 1 newsletter monthly. New survey rounds feed the pipeline continuously.

## Suggested next step
Send the cleared proof numbers and pick a launch date — we finalize the first 3 clusters to 5/5 and hand the visual kit to design.
