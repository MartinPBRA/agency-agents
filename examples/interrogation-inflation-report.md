# Interrogation: "Impact of Inflation on CPC and CPM — 2025 (vs 2024)"

> **Exercise type:** Single-agent deep interrogation
> **Agent deployed:** [Insight Interrogator](../marketing/marketing-insight-interrogator.md)
> **Input:** A competent internal agency report stating that 2025 digital ad
> inflation ran ~12–15.6% (base inflation ~2–3% + CAD depreciation ~3.5% +
> media-industry inflation ~8–10%+), recommending a 10–15% budget increase.
> **Purpose:** Show what the agent adds to a "fine" report before it goes client-facing.

---

## Verdict

This report is **accurate, readable, and not yet client-ready.** It explains
*that* costs rose and offers a tidy decomposition — but it never interrogates
its own headline numbers, and it leans on one convenient story: **"spend up,
results flat? That's inflation, not inefficiency."** That sentence is the most
dangerous line in the document, because it pre-loads an excuse a client will
eventually test. The single biggest question it dodges: **how much of the
increase did *we* cause** (Smart Bidding, broad match, mix shift) versus the
market? Until that's answered, the "ask for 10–15% more budget" recommendation
is resting on an unexamined foundation.

---

## The 3 Questions That Reframe Everything (Rung 4–5)

1. **🔴 R5 — Is the answer "more budget," or "new pricing and new KPIs"?**
   The report frames inflation as a budget problem ("add 10–15%"). But it also
   says ~8–10%+ of the rise is *structural* media-industry inflation that isn't
   reverting. If a permanent slice of cost has moved, the real question isn't
   how much more to spend this year — it's whether retainer pricing, target
   CPAs, and the KPIs we promised clients still make sense in a permanently more
   expensive auction. *Stake: we may be asking for a budget bump when we should
   be renegotiating the engagement.*

2. **🟠 R4 — How much of this is external inflation vs self-inflicted cost?**
   The document attributes the rise entirely to *external* forces (CPI, currency,
   demand). Yet it also names internal-facing drivers — Smart Bidding "willing to
   bid more," broad match expansion, mix shifts toward USD inventory. What share
   of each client's increase is genuinely market-driven versus a consequence of
   *our own* bidding and structure decisions? *Stake: a client who realizes we're
   billing our own auction behavior as "inflation" loses trust instantly.*

3. **🟡 R3 — What did *our* book actually do — not the global average?**
   Every headline here (+12.9% CPC, +15.6% TikTok CPM, +21% travel) is a *global,
   all-industry, third-party* figure. The one number the client cares about —
   what *their* account did, by region and vertical — is absent. If our travel
   clients ran hotter than +21% while our Canada-only search clients were near
   CPI, a single blended "10–15%" is hiding the only insight worth paying for.
   *Stake: the report describes the weather, not our client's house.*

## Numbers Under Oath

| Claim in the report | Missing context | The question to ask first |
|---------------------|-----------------|---------------------------|
| Total ad inflation **"~12–15.6%"** | Range spans 30%; which end applies to *this* client? | "What drives a given account to the top vs bottom of this range — and where does *ours* land?" |
| **CPC +12.9%** globally | Source unnamed; global all-industry, not our accounts | "Is this Google's figure, a vendor's, or ours? What did our managed spend actually do?" |
| **Media-industry inflation "~8–10%+"** | Largest *and* softest component; no source | "Is this measured, or the residual we backed into after subtracting CPI and currency? It's carrying most of the story." |
| **CAD −3.5% vs USD (2025)** | Applies only to USD-billed inventory | "What share of each client's spend is actually USD-denominated? For a Canada-only buyer this term may be ~0." |
| **"15.65% = 12.15% + 3.5%"** | Adds a base-CPC figure (12.15%) not defined elsewhere | "Where did 12.15% come from — and is stacking CPC inflation *and* currency double-counting if the CPC was already quoted in CAD?" |

## Easy Answers We're Leaning On

- **Easy answer:** *"Spend up, results flat? That's inflation, not inefficiency."*
  - **The harder question:** How would we actually *distinguish* inflation from
    inefficiency in a specific account — and have we run that math for this
    client, or are we hoping they accept the macro story instead of auditing
    their own funnel?

- **Easy answer:** *"It's a weak Canadian dollar."*
  - **The harder question:** For a client whose spend is mostly CAD-billed
    domestic inventory, how much of "their" inflation is currency at all — and
    are we applying a portfolio-level narrative to an account it doesn't fit?

- **Easy answer:** *"Just add 10–15% to budget to keep pace."*
  - **The harder question:** Does adding budget into a more expensive, lower-ROI
    auction actually recover results, or just spend more to stand still? At what
    point is the right move to *change channel mix or targets*, not feed the
    auction?

---

## The Full Question Bank (sorted by depth, tagged by type)

- 🔴 **R5 [Consequential]** If 8–10%+ of cost is structural and permanent, should 2026 client KPIs and our retainer model be reset — not just budgets?
- 🔴 **R5 [Consequential]** What's our recommendation when a client *can't* add 10–15% — what do we cut, shift, or re-target instead of asking for more money?
- 🟠 **R4 [Causal]** How much of each client's CPC rise is Smart Bidding / broad match / mix shift (our decisions) vs the market?
- 🟠 **R4 [Causal]** Did *impressions* actually fall (the report cites −15% US search impressions) in our accounts, or is the "inflation" partly a volume story dressed as a price story?
- 🟡 **R3 [Comparative]** What did our book do by region and vertical vs these global averages?
- 🟡 **R3 [Comparative]** Travel ran +21% while "most industries" ran 10–15% — are our travel clients being *under-warned* by the company-wide guidance?
- 🟡 **R3 [Comparative]** CTV CPMs actually *fell* — for clients on CTV, did blended costs really rise, or does the channel mix change the answer entirely?
- 🟡 **R2 [Clarify]** What is the source and method behind the "~8–10%+ media-industry inflation" — the largest single component?
- 🟡 **R2 [Clarify]** Is "12.15% base CPC + 3.5% currency = 15.65%" double-counting, or are those genuinely independent?
- ⚪ **R1 [Restate]** *(table-stakes — flagged, not for the client deck)* Did CPCs and CPMs go up in 2025? Yes.

## What a Sharp Client Will Ask That This Can't Answer Yet

These are gaps the report *raises* but has no data to close. Each is a research brief, not a dead end.

1. **"Show me *my* 2024 vs 2025 CPC/CPM by campaign."** → Pull account-level YoY
   from the ad platforms; this is the deliverable the client actually wants.
2. **"What share of my spend is USD-billed?"** → Quantify currency exposure per
   account so the 3.5% term is applied honestly, not blanket.
3. **"How do you know my flat results are inflation and not the account?"** →
   Decompose CPA change into price (CPC/CPM) vs conversion-rate vs mix, per
   client. This single analysis defends the entire report.
4. **"What's the plan if I don't increase budget?"** → A channel-mix /
   target-reset recommendation, so "add 10–15%" isn't the only answer on offer.

---

### How to read this example

Notice the shift in the **question mix**: the source report lived almost
entirely at Rung 1–2 (it *restates* and lightly *clarifies* what happened). The
interrogation moves the center of gravity to Rung 3–5 — *compared to what,*
*caused by what,* and *so what for the client.* That migration up the depth
ladder is the entire job of the [Insight Interrogator](../marketing/marketing-insight-interrogator.md).
