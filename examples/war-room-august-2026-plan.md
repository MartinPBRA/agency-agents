## 2. Why now — the case from the audit
The May 2026 marketing audit put War Room at **70/100 (B)**. The biggest drag: a **6-month blog gap** (last post Nov 2025) that directly undercuts the "Advertising Institute" positioning and signals inactivity to Google and buyers.

## 7. Ad Grader — Claude-powered pilot
**Concept:** free, self-serve tool. Prospect chooses their path → **Claude scores their paid media** across the same categories as the marketing audit that inspired this plan → delivers a personalized report + score + consult CTA.

**Two entry paths (v1):**
1. **Guided questionnaire** — 8–12 questions on setup, channels, tracking, creative process, reporting. Fast, no login, wide funnel.
2. **Account connect** — via existing Windsor.ai connectors (Google Ads, Meta Ads, GA4). Deeper analysis, sharper report, higher qualification.

**Why Claude:** produces a nuanced narrative report (not just a number), on-brand with People-First — Kedet + Claude assist, a War Room human delivers the final read.
**Pre-qualifies** leads by budget, maturity, channel mix — replaces the generic "free audit" CTA with a data-collecting tool.
**Beyond August:** deeper Kedet integration; embeddable version; benchmarks powered by the September report.
