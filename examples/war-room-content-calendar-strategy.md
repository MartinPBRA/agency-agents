## 2. Deep Response Engine — Laddering the Thin Answers

Each weak input is pushed through *what specifically → why now → contrarian angle → proof → who disagrees*.

### Example A — "Evolving with AI" → a real POV
- **What specifically?** AI in media buying and reporting, not AI as a buzzword.
- **Why now?** Clients are asking what AI changes about *measurement and capacity*.
- **Contrarian angle?** AI doesn't replace the media team — it removes the busywork that was hiding strategic capacity.
- **Proof?** Internal workflow gains; the SkiBig3 BigQuery automation.
- **Who disagrees?** Agencies selling "AI strategists" as headcount.
- 🎯 **Sharpened POV:** *"AI won't replace your media team — it exposes how much of their time you were wasting on reporting. We rebuilt ours around BigQuery and gave the strategy back to the strategists."*

### Example B — "How do we measure DOOH?" → a flagship explainer
- **What specifically?** Attribution and measurement for digital out-of-home.
- **Why now?** It's the #1 prospect question; nobody in the category answers it clearly.
- **Contrarian angle?** Most DOOH "measurement" is impressions theater; real measurement ties exposure to outcomes.
- **Proof?** War Room's own DOOH measurement framework + a client example.
- 🎯 **Sharpened POV:** *"If your DOOH report stops at impressions, you don't have measurement — you have a guess with a logo on it. Here's how we actually attribute it."*

### Example C — "SkiBig3 BigQuery / BC Parks" → proof-driven case content
- These are **proof points**, not topics. Every POV above gets anchored to one of them.

## 3. Content Pillars

Four pillars cover everything the team surfaced, with built-in novelty to honor *"stop talking about the same things."*

1. **DOOH, Demystified** — measurement, attribution, and where DOOH actually fits. *(Answers the top prospect question.)*
2. **Programmatic in Practice** — capabilities, capacity, and how War Room runs accounts. *(Showcases "achievements and capacities.")*
3. **AI, For Real** — honest takes on AI in media ops; no hype. *(The strongest internal opinion, sharpened.)*
4. **Proof & Partnership** — case studies and client-relationship craft (SkiBig3, BC Parks). *(Answers "case studies" + "how we manage client relationships.")*

**Exclusions honored throughout:** no vaping/gambling/regulated-industry content; force novelty so pillars don't recycle the same three posts.

## 7. Distribution & Cadence

- **Primary channel:** LinkedIn (company page + the three named experts' personal profiles).
- **Cascade:** Each pillar piece → carousel → short post → newsletter → short video. (6x reuse per deep idea.)
- **Cadence:** 2–3 posts/week; 1 case study and 1 newsletter per month.
- **Watch & learn from:** Brainlabs and StackAdapt (cited as strong competitor content) — match their depth, beat them on proof and Canadian-market specificity.
- **Inputs to monitor:** Google Trends + Neil Patel for timely angles; AI platform releases for the "AI, For Real" pillar.
